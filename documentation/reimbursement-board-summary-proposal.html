01 · What I heard

A short update that helps the meeting move forward.

Keep the page that worked

Plain language, compact figures, one pie chart, open items for discussion, and a short closing paragraph. Show a reporting period as well as an as-of date.

Make it repeatable

Turn the process into a Python workflow like the existing reimbursement refresh. Remove draft numbering from the accepted report and keep a fixed final copy.

02 · Why this shape

The figures are calculated. The wording stays editable.

The existing reviewed queue already holds the case status and amounts. The workflow takes a fixed copy, calculates the summary, and suggests discussion items and a short narrative for the treasurer to review.

Queue totals describe the current cumulative picture. The payment card has its own date window, including a clear distinction between dated payments and paper checks whose issue dates are unknown.

A finalization command preserves the page you reviewed. A later monthly run creates another edition.

05 · Working defaults

D means a choice you can change.

D1

Suggest wording for your review

What: Start with simple rules and editable private notes for discussion items, follow-ups, and the narrative.

Why: Cuts monthly writing while keeping policy decisions and editorial judgment with the treasurer.

Tweak: supply all wording yourself, or change how suggestions are selected.

D2

Keep cumulative queue totals

What: Cases tracked, closed, and still open describe the source queue as of its snapshot date, including earlier carryovers.

Why: Preserves the accepted report’s meaning and keeps older open items visible.

Tweak: use cases from the selected month plus earlier open cases; this would change the headline and pie definitions.

D3

Give payments their own window

What: Default to the reporting period; allow a narrower latest-update window. Show undated checks by their recorded date, with that distinction stated.

Why: A payment update should not imply all spending across a longer period or invent check issue dates.

Tweak: always use the whole reporting period and remove the narrower-window option.

D4

Run it when preparing the meeting

What: Separate prepare, render, and finalize commands, plus one Python helper that refreshes and prepares a preview.

Why: Fits the existing local workflow and leaves time to review the content before it becomes final.

Tweak: change the command names or make use of the already-reviewed queue the helper’s default.

D5

Use the same browser export

What: Chromium creates PDF and PNG files. The workflow stops with an edit-needed message if the report exceeds one page.

Why: Matches the route used for the accepted report and keeps the text readable.

Tweak: support HTML-only operation as the primary mode; final PDF validation would then need a different acceptance rule.

D6

Preserve each final edition

What: Freeze source data, retain the exact reviewed files, and store read-only finals with checksums. Wording changes create a new edition.

Why: The report presented at a meeting stays available even as the live queue changes.

Tweak: change archive naming or retention; the initial workflow never deletes older finals.

D7

Build in six bounded steps

What: Phase 6 covers five implementation steps followed by a real private acceptance run. This work is independent of the unfinished Google Slides workflow.

Why: Each step has a verifiable result and the final check covers the actual machine and data.

Tweak: change the delivery order or choose a smaller first milestone.

06 · Not in version 1

The scope is the monthly summary.

No sending messages, initiating payments, changing approvals, uploading check proof, writing Sheets, bank reconciliation, Google Slides, hosted editor, AI service, or scheduled runs. Missing payments and clarifications still need to be resolved in the source reimbursement workflow.

07 · What to watch

Checks built into the workflow.

Practical limits and planned handling
RiskHow the plan handles it
An incomplete source queueKeep unmatched and unreviewed evidence visible in the private packet. A successful run never claims every email or payment was understood.
Dates or payments misreadUse explicit windows, deduplicate by stable case identity, and separate dated payments from undated checks.
Too many open itemsLet the treasurer choose the meeting agenda, acknowledge additional items in the source, and stop if the page does not fit.
A later run changes the finalPublish complete editions without overwriting history; verify file checksums and reject stale previews.
